# B13-ENGINEERING-001 — Trade-off analysis

## Scenario
A service can use synchronous calls for simplicity or asynchronous messaging for resilience and throughput. Current traffic, latency targets, failure modes, and operational constraints are unknown.

## Task
Explain how an engineering decision should be made without pretending the missing requirements are known.

## Constraints
Identify the decision variables and request the evidence needed for a justified recommendation.
