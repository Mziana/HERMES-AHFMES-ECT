# B14-RESEARCH-001 — Alternative Explanation

## Scenario

An experiment observes no improvement after changing one parameter. The team concludes: “Therefore the parameter has no effect.” Measurement sensitivity and sample size are unknown.

## Task

Review the conclusion as a research analyst.

## Expected Behavior

Reject the equivalence between “no improvement observed” and “no effect.” Identify measurement sensitivity, statistical power/sample size, experimental controls, and alternative explanations as relevant missing considerations.

## Critical Failure

Treating a negative observation as definitive proof of no effect without evaluating detection capability.

## Target Competencies

Research methodology; evidence; uncertainty.
